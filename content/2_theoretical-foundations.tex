This model assumes that prices $y_t$ at the forecast origin $t$ stay constant throughout the forecast horizon, i.e., for any time offset $h$:\footnote{This is a footnote with supplemental information.}
\[
{\widehat{y}}_{t+h}=y_{t}
\]
